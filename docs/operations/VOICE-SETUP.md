# Voice Setup & Operations Guide

## 1. Microphone & Audio Permissions
1. Open the Sovereign AI Workbench in your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:8300/`.
2. When prompted by the browser, grant microphone access.
3. Click the microphone button `[ 🎤 ]` in the composer or press `Ctrl + Shift + V` to toggle voice input.

## 2. Using Voice with Any Agent
1. Select any of the 101 AI Agents from the sidebar directory.
2. Click `[ 🎤 ]` and speak naturally in Indian English (e.g. *"Explain the working principle of crude distillation columns"*).
3. The live speech will be transcribed into the composer input field.
4. You can edit or append text if needed, then hit `Enter` or click `[ Send ]`.
5. When the AI finishes generating its streamed response, click `[ 🔊 Read Aloud ]` to hear the synthesized spoken response.
6. Click `[ ■ Stop ]` or hit `Escape` at any time to halt speech playback.
