## 3. Naming System

- Every specialist agent `001` to `100` uses an intuitive, technology-oriented role title ending with **`AI Agent`** (e.g. `Writing AI Agent`, `Research AI Agent`, `Coding AI Agent`, `SQL AI Agent`, `Cybersecurity AI Agent`).
- Agent `101` is named **`Super AI Agent`** with description: *"The master AI agent that coordinates the specialist AI agents to solve complex tasks."*
- Strictly **numeric monospace avatars** (`001` to `101`). No letter-based avatars.

---

## 4. Status Terminology

- Allowed status values: `Active`, `Busy`, `Offline`, `Error`.
- `Online` is prohibited across all user-facing indicators.
- Footer badge: `● 101 Agents Active`.
