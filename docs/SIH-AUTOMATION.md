# SOVEREIGN AI WORKBENCH — LOCAL AUTOMATION, SCHEDULER & NOTIFICATIONS

**System:** Sovereign AI Workbench  
**Module:** On-Premise Event Bus, Scheduler & In-App Notification Center  
**Date:** August 22, 2026  

---

## 1. On-Premise Event-Driven Automation

The Local Event Bus enables asynchronous processing of plant events without internet connectivity:
- **`NEW_DOCUMENT`**: Automatically triggers multimodal OCR and table extraction.
- **`NEW_INSPECTION_RECORD`**: Ingests fresh UT scans and updates equipment degradation trends.
- **`THRESHOLD_BREACHED`**: Automatically generates high-priority in-app alerts and flags for HITL review.

---

## 2. In-App Notification Center

Role-filtered notifications route critical action items directly to responsible engineers:
- **Safety Officer:** Approvals required for high-risk statutory operations.
- **Plant Inspector:** Live evidence conflict alerts and missing parameter inquiries.
- **Operations Manager:** Executive briefing readiness notifications.
