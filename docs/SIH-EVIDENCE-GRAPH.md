## 1. Typed Evidence Relationships

The Evidence Graph links disparate pieces of empirical and statutory data using formal semantic edges:

- **`SUPPORTS`**: Confirms or corroborates a finding across independent sources.
- **`CONTRADICTS`**: Highlights discrepancies (e.g. 5.90 mm vs 6.80 mm), escalating to HITL review.
- **`DERIVED_FROM`**: Establishes formula provenance (e.g. remaining life derived from UT readings and API-653).
- **`REFERS_TO`**: Cites authoritative clauses in refinery standards.
- **`SUPERSEDES`**: Replaces historical baseline measurements with current verified NDT scans.

## 2. Source Authority Hierarchy

When resolving conflicts between records, the engine applies weighted authority scoring:

$$\text{Official Statutory SOP (100)} > \text{Technical Spec (80)} > \text{NDT Report (70)} > \text{Historical Log (50)} > \text{Informal Note (30)}$$
